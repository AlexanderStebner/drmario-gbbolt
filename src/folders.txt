# Virtual folders, in reading order: path | description
# Functions say where they live with `;@ path: folder/sub`; data blocks without a
# path go to the folder of the functions that use them.
boot | Power-on, interrupt vectors and the main loop that runs one game state per frame.
boot/vectors | The fixed addresses the CPU jumps to: restarts, interrupts, the cartridge entry point.
boot/header | The cartridge header: logo, title, cartridge type, checksums.
system | Hardware services used everywhere.
system/interrupts | The VBlank, timer and LCD interrupt handlers.
system/link | The link cable: serial interrupt, byte exchange.
system/lcd | Switching the LCD off, the OAM DMA routine in HRAM.
system/vblank | Drawing jobs the VBlank handler runs for the game: a new virus, a letter of the ending text, bottle rows.
system/input | Reading the joypad.
lib | Small general routines.
lib/memory | Copying memory blocks.
lib/math | BCD arithmetic.
lib/timing | Busy-wait delays.
gfx | Getting things onto the screen.
gfx/tiles | Loading tile graphics into VRAM.
gfx/tilemaps | Background maps: whole screens, blocks, single tiles.
gfx/objects | Sprites: object records, drawing them into the shadow OAM, blinking cursors.
gfx/numbers | Drawing BCD numbers.
screens | The screens around the game itself.
screens/title | The title screen.
screens/demo | The title demo: a fixed bottle, fixed capsules and recorded button presses.
screens/options | Virus level, speed and music selection, for one player and over the link cable.
game | Clearing viruses: one player, or each side of a link game.
game/setup | Starting a level: screen, sprites, drop speed, shared capsule list.
game/viruses | Filling the bottle with viruses.
game/capsules | Dealing capsules: the random colours, the next capsule.
game/play | The playing state: moving, rotating and dropping capsules.
game/lines | Finding four in a row, clearing them, letting halves fall.
game/score | Points and the virus counters on the side panel.
game/magnifier | The magnifying glass on the 1-player screen: the three big viruses and their animations.
game/end | Clearing the level or topping out.
versus | The 2-player game over the link cable.
versus/setup | Sharing the bottle and capsules before a round.
versus/play | A round: exchanging garbage and results.
versus/results | Round and match results, the win markers.
cutscenes | The underwater cutscenes: on HI speed after levels 5, 10 and 15, and an ending for each speed after level 20.
sound | The sound engine (runs once per frame from VBlank).
sound/api | The engine's entry points and the per-frame update.
sound/sfx | Sound effects on the square and noise channels.
sound/sfx/common | Starting effects, writing channel registers, effect timing.
sound/sfx/square | Square channel effects 1-14.
sound/sfx/noise | Noise channel effects 1-3.
sound/music | The music sequencer: songs, patterns with repeats, notes, instruments, vibrato, panning.
sound/music/songs | The song data: per song a header, a pattern list per channel, the patterns.
